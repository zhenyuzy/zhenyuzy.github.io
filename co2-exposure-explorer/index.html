Models and calculations

Inter-trip model: next-trip initial CO₂ is estimated from previous-trip end CO₂, parking duration, and indoor/outdoor parking, with vehicle represented by a random intercept during model fitting. The webpage uses fixed-effect predictions for an average vehicle.

Within-trip model: the unified 0–60 min GAMM predicts minute-level CO₂ from initial CO₂, occupancy, ventilation, vehicle type, and their time-varying contributions. Vehicle and trip random effects are excluded from webpage predictions.

Trip exposure is integrated using the trapezoidal rule. Parking affects the next initial concentration but is not included in the in-vehicle exposure total. Warnings identify sparse combinations and extrapolation beyond observed joint support.

Exposure context

Office-workday reference

Approximately 200,000 ppm·min accumulated during a typical office workday was reported by Gall et al. (2016). The “office-workday exposure equivalents” metric divides the scenario total by this reference value.

Gall, E. T., Cheung, T., Luhung, I., Schiavon, S., & Nazaroff, W. W. (2016). Real-time monitoring of personal exposures to carbon dioxide. Building and Environment, 104, 59–67. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.buildenv.2016.04.021
